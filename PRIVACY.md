# Privacy Policy — Karaoke Studio for YouTube

_Last updated: 2026-10-19_

## English

Karaoke Studio for YouTube does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

- **Audio**: When you start processing, the extension captures the audio of the YouTube tab you chose and processes it locally in your browser (key, speed, channel, EQ and key detection). The audio is never recorded, saved or uploaded.
- **Settings**: Your settings (key shift, speed, channel mode, EQ, volume, pitch engine, panel on/off) are saved with `chrome.storage.local` on your own device only.
- **Web pages**: A content script runs only on youtube.com to change the video's playback speed and show the optional control panel. It does not read or collect page content, browsing history or account information.
- **Network**: The extension makes no network requests of its own. There is no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party service.
- **Permissions**: `activeTab`, `tabCapture`, `offscreen` and `storage`, plus access to youtube.com, are used only for the features above.

Uninstalling the extension removes its saved settings.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/unickhow/karaoke_studio_for_youtube/issues

## 繁體中文

Karaoke Studio for YouTube 不會收集、儲存、傳送或出售任何個人資料。

- **音訊**：開始處理後，擴充功能會擷取你所選 YouTube 分頁的音訊，並在你的瀏覽器裡處理（變調、變速、聲道、等化與調性辨識）。音訊不會被錄下、儲存或上傳。
- **設定**：你的設定（Key、速度、聲道模式、等化、音量、變調引擎、面板開關）只透過 `chrome.storage.local` 存在你自己的裝置上。
- **網頁**：內容腳本只在 youtube.com 執行，用來調整影片播放速度並顯示選用的控制面板；不會讀取或收集網頁內容、瀏覽紀錄或帳號資訊。
- **網路**：擴充功能本身不發出任何網路請求，沒有分析、追蹤、廣告或第三方服務。
- **權限**：`activeTab`、`tabCapture`、`offscreen`、`storage` 與 youtube.com 存取權限，只用於上述功能。

移除擴充功能後，儲存的設定也會一併刪除。

問題回報：https://github.com/unickhow/karaoke_studio_for_youtube/issues
